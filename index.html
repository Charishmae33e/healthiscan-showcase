MediCore Innovations is a healthcare technology startup that develops smart digital solutions to improve the efficiency, accessibility, and organization of healthcare services. Its featured project, HealthiScan: Smart Vital Signs Kiosk with Queuing System, is designed for Barangay Graceville Health Center.

✳
THE IDEA

Improve the initial healthcare experience.

HealthiScan supports patient identification, vital signs assessment, BMI computation, and queue management through one integrated system. It aims to help organize the initial assessment process in community healthcare facilities.

◎
OUR FOCUS

Accessible digital healthcare.

The system combines RFID-based patient identification, digital patient records, vital signs measurement, and web-based interfaces for Barangay Health Workers, healthcare providers, and IT administrators.

↗
THE INNOVATION

A more organized patient journey.

HealthiScan generates service-specific queue numbers, supports priority categories, prints queue slips, and provides voice and visual queue announcements to help organize patient flow.
